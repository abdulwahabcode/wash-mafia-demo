About us

Broadmeadows' detailing crew

The Wash Mafia is a local car detailing crew based at 80 Camp Road, Broadmeadows. We're car people — the kind who notice the swirl marks, the dust in the vents, the tyre shine that most shops skip.

We started because Melbourne's north deserved a detailer that treats a daily driver with the same care as a show car. Fair prices, honest advice, and finishes that turn heads in the driveway.
